## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)

EdgeFolio by Jenix brings attendance, access control, payroll and HR together — and works with the
attendance machine you already have.

ATTENDANCE FROM ANYWHERE
• Mark attendance from your phone with a face check and live location
• Office, on tour or working from home — your HR decides what is allowed
• Works without internet: punches are saved and sent when you are back online
• See today's status, your monthly calendar and attendance history

ANY MACHINE, ONE RECORD
• Face, fingerprint and card machines from many brands feed the same attendance record
• Phone punches and machine punches appear side by side
• Temporary workers can get an ID automatically at a visitor machine — no enrollment forms

FOR OWNERS AND HR
• Live view of who is in, late or absent, across every site
• Approve leave, missed punches and field visits from the app
• Office locations with a radius, so office punches are made at the office
• Push alerts for late arrivals and pending approvals
• Roles for owner, HR admin and employee

PAYROLL AND HR
• Attendance flows straight into salary calculation in EdgeFolio for Windows
• Salary structures, PF / ESI / PT, salary slips and leave — kept on your own office PC
• Employees see their salary slips and leave balance in the app

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Faces are stored as numbers, never as photos
• Each company's data is kept separate and is never shared
• Your payroll stays on your office computer

Get started in minutes: create your company with Google sign-in, add your team, and share your
company code. Already using EdgeFolio on Windows? Connect it under Settings → Cloud Attendance and
use the same company code.

Made in India by Jenix (IOTSoft).

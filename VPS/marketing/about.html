About EDGEFOLIO

EDGEFOLIO is an offline-first payroll and attendance platform designed for Indian SMEs, factories, field teams, and multi-branch businesses that need reliable HR operations even with unstable internet.

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Our Mission

Our mission is to make payroll and attendance management simple, transparent, and affordable for businesses that do not want to depend on always-on cloud connectivity.

EDGEFOLIO runs on local infrastructure so your core records stay available during network issues and power-fluctuation scenarios.

What We Build

  • Attendance tracking with multi-source input support
  • Payroll calculation with India-focused compliance workflows
  • Leave, expenses, and reporting modules for daily operations
  • Optional cloud sync and backup for remote monitoring

Who We Serve

  • Factories and manufacturing units
  • Retail chains and local franchises
  • Field-service businesses
  • SMEs transitioning from manual registers and spreadsheets

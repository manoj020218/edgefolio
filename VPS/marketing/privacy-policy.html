Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how EDGEFOLIO collects, uses, stores, and protects data when you use our software, website, and related services.

Effective date: April 28, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to EDGEFOLIO desktop software, companion mobile app, and the public website at edgefolio.iotsoft.in.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • Organization details, employee records, attendance data, and payroll inputs
  • Support requests sent by email

b) Device or usage data

  • Basic technical logs required for reliability and troubleshooting
  • Backup and sync metadata (for configured cloud sync users)

c) Sensitive data

  • If biometric or face-based attendance features are enabled, templates may be processed and stored by your configured deployment
  • Raw biometric media should be minimized according to your compliance policy

3. How We Use Data

  • To provide payroll, attendance, leave, and reporting functionality
  • To maintain service stability and customer support
  • To improve product performance and security
  • To satisfy legal or regulatory obligations when applicable

4. Data Storage and Retention

EDGEFOLIO is designed as an offline-first platform. Most operational records are stored on your local edge node.

Retention duration is controlled by your organization settings and deployment policy. You are responsible for retention and deletion practices required by local law.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your authorized administrators and configured team users
  • With infrastructure providers needed to deliver optional sync/backup services
  • When required by law, court order, or lawful authority

6. Security Measures

  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Backup encryption and controlled sync channels where configured
  • Operational logging for anomaly detection and troubleshooting

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of relevant personal data. Please contact us to initiate requests.

8. Children's Privacy

EDGEFOLIO is a business application and is not intended for children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest revision date will always be shown on this page.

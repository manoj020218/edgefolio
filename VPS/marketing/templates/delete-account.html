Delete your EdgeFolio account

EdgeFolio by Jenix (Android app)  ·  EdgeFolio Cloud

ℹ️

EdgeFolio by Jenix is published by IOTSoft. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time, free of charge. We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

1
How to request deletion

Send a request from the e-mail address or phone number linked to your account, with:

  • your company code (shown on the app's login screen),
  • your Employee ID (or, for an owner, the Google e-mail used to sign in),
  • whether you want to delete only your own account or the whole company (owners only).

✉️ E-mail: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} (subject: "Delete my EdgeFolio account")
💬 WhatsApp: {{SUPPORT_WHATSAPP}}

Employees can also ask their company's HR/owner, who can deactivate the account straight away; deletion of the stored data is then done by us on request.

2
What we delete
  • Your account details (name, Employee ID, role, e-mail/phone, Google account link)
  • Your face signature (Face ID)
  • Your attendance records, punch locations and work assignments
  • Your notifications inbox and push token
  • For a whole-company request: every employee account and record of that company, and its licence registration
3
What we may keep, and for how long
  • Encrypted server backups are overwritten within a further 30 days after deletion.
  • A minimal audit entry (that a deletion was done, and when) may be kept to show we handled the request.
  • Records your company already copied to its own office PC (EdgeFolio for Windows) are stored by your company, not by us — ask your employer to remove them there.
